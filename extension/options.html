No New Tabs

Pages on listed sites cannot open new tabs or windows. One rule per line:

  • example.com: the site and its subdomains (www.example.com, app.example.com)
  • =app.example.com: that exact host only
  • *: every site
  • !docs.example.com: an exception, never listed (with its subdomains; !=host for one exact host). Exceptions beat every other rule.

* plus exceptions is an allowlist: new tabs are blocked everywhere except on the sites you except. Adding a site takes effect at once; reload open tabs after removing one.
